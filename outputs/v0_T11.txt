Soft paws tread on silent floors,
A shadow slips through open doors.
With amber eyes that gleam like gold,
A story of the night is told.

A gentle purr, a rhythmic hum,
To where the weary spirits come.
With velvet ears and whiskers long,
They sing a quiet, sleepy song.

They stretch beneath the morning sun,
Their playful hunting now is done.
A curled-up ball of fur and grace,
The masters of this quiet space.
